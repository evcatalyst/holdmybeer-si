Secure Peering

Nodes discover each other with Bonjour and publish signed capability advertisements. Mutual authentication establishes a robust peer ring, analogous to NTP but cryptographically secured. Each Mac declares its identity, unified memory, models, and current state.

Capacity Leasing

The local terminal owns the session. It requests leases from an allocator that never sees prompts or tokens. Venues enforce reservations directly. A five-Mac group becomes one exclusive execution venue for distributed models over Thunderbolt 5 RDMA.

Native Execution & Isolation

MLX and exo deliver native Apple Silicon performance. Apple Container micro-VMs provide hypervisor isolation, with vsock bridges preserving near-native Metal throughput. Resource limits and dedicated users keep the interactive desktop responsive.

Enterprise Fabric

Macs replace thin clients. Local mesh inference keeps data on-premises, reduces token spend, and scales with M5 Pro fleets under MDM and Apple Business Manager. Direct connections preserve privacy and latency.
