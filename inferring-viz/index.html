InferRing

Distributed Inference Fabric for Apple Silicon Macs

Secure peer mesh that turns home and office Macs into a private, low-latency inference cluster. Reduce token spend, keep data local, leverage native hardware.

Peers register with signed capability ads (NTP-like). Master distributes inference work. Isolation via Apple Containers. RDMA/TB5 for large models.

Secure Peering & Identity

Bonjour discovery + Ed25519/Secure Enclave identities. Mutual mTLS. Capability advertisements: memory, chip, models, load, power state.

Distributed Scheduling

Condor ClassAd-inspired matchmaking + Minions pull-based opportunistic scavenging. Watchdog for sleep/failure. Workers can spill to external.

Native Execution & Ring-Fence

MLX / exo for Apple Silicon. Apple Container micro-VMs for isolation (with vsock MLX bridge ~95% perf). Dedicated users, resource limits.

Enterprise Play

Replace thin clients with Macs that provide local distributed inference. MDM + Apple Business Manager. Data residency, lower latency, zero external tokens for routine work. Scales with M5 Pro fleets.
